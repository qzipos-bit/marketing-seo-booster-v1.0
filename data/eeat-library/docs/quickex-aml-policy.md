# Quickex AML / KYC Policy (official)

**URL:** https://quickex.io/docs/aml-policy  
**Tier:** T1_site · **Pillar:** T

## Key quotes

> The exchange office, as a service provider of virtual/cryptocurrency assets, is obliged to verify all suspicious transactions. During verification it is necessary to carry out user identification (KYC) and establish the origin of the virtual/cryptocurrency asset.

> Asset refunds on transactions with erroneous transfers as well as AML-related checks and blocks are **not possible without full KYC verification**.

> When sending assets from the liquidity provider, the initiator of the blockchain has the right to refuse to pay out frozen funds.

- Contradicts marketing «no KYC» on privacy pairs → **EE23 overclaim risk**
