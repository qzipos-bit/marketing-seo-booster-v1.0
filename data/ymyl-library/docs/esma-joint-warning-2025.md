# Joint ESAs — Revised Warning on Crypto-assets (Oct 2025)

**URL:** https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-10/Joint_ESAs_revised_warning_on_crypto-assets.pdf

## Key quotes

> Only firms **authorised and listed on the ESMA register** are allowed to provide crypto-asset services in the EU under MiCA.

> Transitional period applies until **July 2026** — you will **not benefit from MiCA protections** until provider is authorised.
