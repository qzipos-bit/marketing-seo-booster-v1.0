# Demotion Signals & Quality Filters

> Compiled from leaked Content Warehouse modules + analyst crosswalks (iPullRank, Optimal, Growfusely).

## Categories of demotion referenced in leak analysis

### Content quality / Helpful Content lineage

- Thin, scaled, or affiliate-heavy pages without first-hand value
- Attributes tied to "helpful content" style demotions in analyst mappings
- **Crypto context:** March 2024 spam updates hit low-trust crypto sites hard

### Navigation & UX demotion

- "Poor navigational experience" — confusing IA, orphan money pages
- **Fix:** Footer links to policies, support, fees; breadcrumbs on pair pages

### Link-based demotion

- Irrelevant or mismatched anchor profiles
- Spam backlink velocity (ties to Ahrefs weekly report)
- **Fix:** Disavow only after human review; focus on earning relevant PR links

### Exact-match domain (EMD)

- `exactMatchDomainDemotion` style attributes in leak indexes
- Less relevant for established brand domains like quickex.io

### User dissatisfaction

- Overlaps NavBoost bad clicks
- Porn, hate, or deceptive content classifiers (YMYL adjacent)

## Google Quality Rater Guidelines (official — not a leak, but operational rules)

The QRG is the human raters' manual. Leak attributes often **encode what raters measure**:

| QRG section | Leak theme |
|-------------|------------|
| §2.3 YMYL | Financial harm, crypto investment risk |
| §3.4 E-E-A-T | Experience, expertise, authority, trust |
| Lowest PQ | Scam, no ownership, no policies |

**Official PDF:** https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf
